## Down jackets

Down loses loft when it is dirty, so washing a down jacket properly makes it warmer, not weaker.

- Wash cold, on a gentle cycle, with a down-specific detergent. Regular detergent strips the natural oils from the down, and fabric softener makes it clump.
- Close all zippers and empty the pockets first.
- Tumble dry low with two or three clean tennis balls. The balls break up clumps and restore the loft. Drying takes a while, often two hours or more.
- Make sure the jacket is completely dry before you put it away, and store it hanging or loosely folded, not compressed in its stuff sack.

## Tents

- Never machine wash a tent. The agitation damages the waterproof coating and the seam tape. Clean it by hand with a sponge, cold water, and a mild non-detergent soap.
- Dry the tent fully before storage. Packing it away damp leads to mildew and a breakdown of the coating, which warranty does not cover.
- Shake out dirt and grit before packing, and use a footprint to protect the floor.
- Store it loosely in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight.

## Boots

- Re-waterproof your boots every 3-4 months of regular use, or sooner if water stops beading on the surface.
- Brush off mud after each hike. Dried mud pulls moisture out of leather and makes it crack.
- Clean the boots before applying a waterproofing treatment, and apply it while they are still slightly damp.
- Dry boots at room temperature with the insoles removed. Never put them next to a heater, fire, or in a dryer, because heat weakens the glue and shrinks leather.

## Why care matters for warranty

Fernhill-brand gear has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects, and tents and backpacks have 3 years. Damage caused by washing or storing gear against these instructions is not a manufacturing defect, so it is not covered. Normal wear and tear is not covered either.
