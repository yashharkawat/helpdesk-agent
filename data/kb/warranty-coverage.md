## What is covered

Fernhill-brand gear comes with a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Tents and backpacks have a longer 3-year warranty, because they are built to last through many seasons. The warranty period starts on the delivery date of your order.

A manufacturing defect is a fault in materials or workmanship. Typical examples:

- Seams that come apart without being overloaded.
- Zippers that separate or lose teeth under normal use.
- Tent poles that crack at a joint during normal pitching.
- Waterproof coatings or seam tape that peel off early.
- Buckles, snaps, or strap stitching that fail.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear is not covered. Gear that gets used will show it, and that is not a defect. The warranty also does not cover:

- Worn-down boot soles, faded fabric, or pilling.
- Damage from accidents, misuse, fire, animals, or improper storage, such as mildew on a tent packed away wet.
- Damage from washing or drying against the care instructions.
- Alterations or repairs made by someone else.
- Products from other brands that we sell. Those carry the maker's own warranty, and we can point you to the right contact.

## Warranty versus returns

A return is for unused gear with tags, within 30 days of delivery for Standard members or 60 days for Plus members. A warranty claim is for gear that has been used and failed because of a defect, within the 1-year or 3-year period. If your item arrived already damaged, use the damaged on arrival process instead, which must be reported within 14 days of delivery.

## Final-sale items

Fernhill-brand gear bought as final sale still carries the same warranty. Final sale limits returns, not defect coverage.

To make a claim you need photos and your order number. See the article on how to file a warranty claim for the steps.
