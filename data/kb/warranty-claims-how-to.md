## Before you start

Check that your item is covered. Fernhill-brand gear has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects, and tents and backpacks have a 3-year warranty, counted from the delivery date. Normal wear and tear is not covered. If you are not sure whether your problem counts as a defect, file the claim anyway and a specialist will assess it.

Every warranty claim needs two things:

- Photos of the problem.
- The order number, which starts with "FH-". You can find it in your confirmation email or under "Orders" in your account.

We cannot open a claim without both.

## Taking useful photos

Good photos speed up the decision. Please include:

1. One photo of the whole item.
2. One or two close-ups of the defect, in focus and in good light.
3. A photo of the product label or tag inside the item, if it has one.

For a leaking seam or a failed coating, a photo showing where the water gets in helps. Clean the item first so the defect is visible.

## Submitting the claim

Contact support through chat at any time. The AI assistant collects your order number, a short description of what happened, and your photos, then passes the claim to a human specialist for assessment. Specialists work Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm Mountain Time, and reply within 1 business day.

## What happens next

If the claim is approved, we repair or replace the item. If the same item is no longer available, we offer a comparable item or a refund of the price you paid. Refunds go to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear after approval. If we need the item back for inspection, we send a free prepaid label.

If the claim is declined, for example because the damage is normal wear and tear, the specialist explains why and may suggest a repair option.
