## Where your refund goes

Every refund is sent to the original payment method used on the order. If you paid by credit or debit card, the money returns to that card. If you paid with PayPal, it returns to your PayPal account. If you paid with a Fernhill gift card, the amount is restored to the gift card balance. We cannot redirect a refund to a different card, a different person, or a bank transfer.

If you split payment between a gift card and another method, each portion goes back to where it came from.

## How long it takes

Once a refund is approved, it takes 5-7 business days to appear on your statement. Business days are Monday to Friday and do not include public holidays. Approval is the starting point, not the day you asked for the refund or the day you dropped off a return.

You will get an email the moment the refund is approved. The email shows the refund amount and the order number.

## Refunds that need a specialist

The AI assistant can approve refunds up to USD 100.00 per order on its own, usually within the same conversation. Refunds above USD 100.00 are escalated to a human specialist, who replies within 1 business day. The 5-7 business day bank timeline begins after the specialist approves the refund, so larger refunds can take slightly longer end to end.

## If your refund has not arrived

Count 7 full business days from the approval email before contacting us. Some banks show a refund as a reversal of the original charge rather than a new line, so check the original transaction date on your statement as well.

If nothing has appeared after 7 business days, contact support with your order number. We will confirm the refund date and reference so you can follow up with your bank or card issuer.

Standard members who used a paid return label will see the flat USD 7.95 return shipping fee deducted from the refund amount.
