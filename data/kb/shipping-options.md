## Standard shipping

Standard shipping takes 4-6 business days. It is free on orders over USD 75. On orders of USD 75 or less it costs USD 6.95. The threshold is based on the item subtotal after discounts and before tax.

## Express shipping

Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs USD 14.95. Plus members get free Express shipping on orders over USD 150. Express is a good choice when you need gear before a specific trip, but keep the processing cut-off in mind: only orders placed before 1 pm Mountain Time ship the same business day. An Express order placed at 3 pm on Friday ships on Monday and arrives on Wednesday.

## Where we ship

We ship only within the United States and Canada. We cannot ship to other countries or to freight forwarders that re-export packages. Orders to Canada add 3-5 business days to the times above, and any duties or import taxes are paid by the customer. See the Canada shipping article for details.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with UPS, USPS, and FedEx. The carrier is chosen by the warehouse based on your address and the package size, so you cannot select one at checkout. When your order status changes to "shipped", you receive an email with the carrier name and a tracking number. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show its first scan.

- Business days are Monday to Friday and exclude public holidays.
- Delivery estimates start on the ship date, not the order date.
- Large orders may ship in more than one box. Each box has its own tracking number.
- Your card is charged when the order ships.

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, see the lost or missing package article for what to do next.
