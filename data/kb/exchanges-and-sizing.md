## We do not do direct exchanges

Fernhill Outfitters does not offer direct exchanges. If you need a different size, color, or model, return the item for a refund and place a new order for the one you want. We handle it this way because stock moves quickly, and holding an item while another travels back to us often ends with the replacement selling out.

## The fastest way to swap

1. Place the new order now, so the size you want is reserved.
2. Contact support with the original order number to start the return.
3. Send the original item back unused and with its tags.
4. Your refund goes to the original payment method and takes 5-7 business days to appear after approval.

The return must be inside your window: 30 days from delivery for Standard members, 60 days for Plus members. Items marked "Final sale" cannot be returned, so they cannot be swapped for another size either.

## Footwear size exchanges

Boots and shoes are the hardest items to size online, so a size exchange on footwear gets a free return label for everyone, Standard and Plus alike. Tell support that you are returning footwear for a different size and the flat USD 7.95 return fee is waived. You still place the new order yourself.

For other items, Plus members always get a free label, and Standard members pay the flat USD 7.95 deducted from the refund.

## Getting the size right

- Each product page has a size chart with measurements in inches and centimeters. Measure yourself rather than relying on your size in another brand.
- For boots, measure your feet in the afternoon while wearing the socks you hike in. If you are between sizes, go up half a size.
- For backpacks, fit depends on torso length, not height. The size chart explains how to measure it.
- Try items on indoors, on a clean floor, and keep the tags on until you are sure.
