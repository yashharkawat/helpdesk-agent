## Return windows

You can return most Fernhill Outfitters items for a refund. How long you have depends on your membership tier:

- Standard members: 30 days from the date of delivery.
- Plus members: 60 days from the date of delivery.

The window starts on the day the carrier marks your package as delivered, not the day you placed the order. If your order is still in transit, the clock has not started yet.

## Condition requirements

To qualify for a return, the item must be unused and still have its original tags attached. Trying on a jacket or a pair of boots indoors is fine. Gear that has been worn on the trail, washed, or altered does not qualify for a standard return. If something failed during use, check our warranty coverage article instead, because a manufacturing defect is handled as a warranty claim rather than a return.

## What cannot be returned

Items marked "Final sale" (typically clearance items) cannot be returned or refunded. The only exception is a final-sale item that arrives damaged or defective. Gift cards and Plus membership fees are covered by their own rules, described in the gift cards and Plus membership articles.

## How to start a return

1. Have your order number ready. It starts with "FH-" and appears in your confirmation email.
2. Contact support through chat. The AI assistant is available 24/7 and can check eligibility right away.
3. Once the return is approved, you will receive a return shipping label by email. Plus members get a free prepaid label. Standard members pay a flat USD 7.95, which is deducted from the refund.
4. Pack the item with its tags and drop it off with the carrier shown on the label.

Your refund goes back to your original payment method and takes 5-7 business days to appear after approval. We do not offer direct exchanges, so if you need a different size or color, return the item and place a new order.
