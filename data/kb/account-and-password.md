## Resetting your password

1. Go to the sign-in page and select "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the reset email and follow the link.
4. Choose a new password.

The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. After that it expires and you need to request a new one. If you request several links, only the most recent one works.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder and confirm you entered the address you signed up with. For security reasons, support cannot set or tell you a password, and neither our team nor the AI assistant will ever ask for your current one.

## Updating your details

Sign in and open "Account settings" to change your name, email address, saved addresses, and communication preferences. Changing a saved address only affects future orders. To change the address on an existing order, contact support while the order is still "processing".

If you change your email address, we send a confirmation link to the new address. The change takes effect once you confirm it.

## Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account by contacting support from the email address on the account. Account deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Before you ask, note that:

- Deletion is permanent. Your order history, saved addresses, and any active Plus membership are removed.
- Open orders, returns, and warranty claims should be finished first, because we cannot look up a deleted account.
- Gift card balances are not tied to your account. Keep the gift card emails and codes, since gift cards never expire.
- We keep some transaction records where the law requires it, such as tax records.

## Locked accounts

After several failed sign-in attempts, the account locks for a short time to protect you. Wait 15 minutes and then reset your password using the steps above.
