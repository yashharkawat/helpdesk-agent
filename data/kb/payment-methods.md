## What we accept

You can pay for a Fernhill Outfitters order with:

- Major credit and debit cards.
- PayPal.
- Fernhill gift cards.

You can combine one or more gift cards with a card or PayPal on the same order. We do not accept cash on delivery, checks, money orders, or bank transfers. All prices and charges are in US dollars, including orders shipped to Canada.

## When you are charged

Your card is charged when the order ships, not when you place it. While the order status is "processing", you may see a pending authorization on your statement. That is a temporary hold to confirm the card is valid, not a charge. If you cancel while the order is processing, the hold drops off on its own within a few business days.

Gift card balances are deducted as soon as you place the order. If the order is cancelled, the balance is restored to the gift card.

The USD 39 yearly Plus fee can be paid by credit or debit card or PayPal. Gift cards cannot be used to buy Plus membership.

## Declined payments

If your payment is declined, check the following:

- The billing address matches the one your bank has on file.
- The card number, expiry date, and security code are correct.
- The card has not expired and has enough available credit.
- Your bank has not blocked the transaction. Some banks flag a first purchase with a new retailer.

If the card is declined at the moment of shipping, the order stays in "processing" and we email you to update the payment method.

## Refunds

Refunds always go to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear after approval. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

For your security, never send full card numbers to support by chat or email. Our team and the AI assistant will never ask for them.
