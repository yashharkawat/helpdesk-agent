## The basics

Fernhill gift cards are digital. They are delivered by email to the recipient you choose, with a 16-character code and the balance. Three rules apply to every gift card:

- Gift cards never expire. There are no inactivity fees and the balance does not shrink over time.
- Gift cards are not refundable for cash. Once bought, a gift card cannot be returned, and an unused balance cannot be paid out.
- Gift cards cannot be used to buy Plus membership. Plus must be paid for with a credit or debit card or PayPal.

## Using a gift card

Enter the code in the "Gift card" field at checkout. The balance is applied to your order total, including shipping. If the order costs more than the balance, pay the rest with a card or PayPal. If it costs less, the remaining balance stays on the gift card for next time. You can apply more than one gift card to a single order.

To check a balance, sign in, open "Gift cards" in your account, and enter the code.

## Returns on gift card orders

Refunds go to the original payment method. If you paid with a gift card, the refunded amount is restored to that gift card, not paid in cash or to a bank card. If you split payment, each portion goes back where it came from. Keep the gift card email even after the balance reaches zero, in case you return something later.

## Lost codes and problems

If you lose the email, contact support with the purchaser's email address and the approximate purchase date. We can resend the gift card to the original recipient address. We cannot replace a balance that has already been spent, so treat the code like cash and do not share it.

Gift cards bought during a sale do not qualify for price adjustments, because a gift card's value does not change.
