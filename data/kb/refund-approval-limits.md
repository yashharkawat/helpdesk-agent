## What the AI assistant can approve

Our support assistant is an AI agent that is available 24/7. It can look up your order, check whether it qualifies for a refund, and approve the refund itself when the amount is USD 100.00 or less per order. In those cases you normally get a decision in the same conversation, with no waiting for a person.

The assistant still applies every policy rule before approving anything. The order must be inside your return window (30 days from delivery for Standard members, 60 days for Plus members), the item must be unused with tags, and the item must not be marked "Final sale" unless it arrived damaged or defective.

## What needs a human specialist

Any refund above USD 100.00 on a single order must be escalated to a human specialist. The assistant cannot approve it, split it into smaller refunds, or promise an outcome. It will collect the details, open the escalation, and tell you what happens next.

A specialist replies within 1 business day. Specialists work Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm Mountain Time, so a request sent on Friday evening is picked up on Monday.

## Why the limit exists

Larger refunds get a second look from a person so that mistakes are caught before money moves. The limit is about who approves the refund, not whether you qualify. An eligible refund above USD 100.00 is still paid in full once a specialist signs off.

## After approval

Whoever approves it, the refund goes to your original payment method and takes 5-7 business days to appear. You receive a confirmation email with the amount and your order number. If a request is declined, the assistant or the specialist explains which rule applied, for example an expired return window or a final-sale item.
