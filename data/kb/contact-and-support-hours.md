## Two ways to get help

Fernhill Outfitters support has two parts: an AI assistant and a team of human specialists.

The AI assistant is available 24/7, every day of the year, through the chat window on any page of our site. Human specialists work Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm Mountain Time. They are closed on weekends and public holidays.

You can also email support@fernhill-outfitters.example.com. Emails are answered by specialists during the hours above.

## What the AI assistant can do

The assistant can handle most requests from start to finish:

- Look up an order and its tracking.
- Cancel an order or change its address while the status is "processing".
- Check return eligibility and send a return label.
- Approve refunds up to USD 100.00 per order.
- Answer questions about shipping, warranty, Plus membership, and product care.

## When a specialist takes over

Some requests must be escalated to a human specialist:

- Any refund above USD 100.00 on a single order.
- Warranty claim assessments.
- Anything the assistant is not sure about, or when you ask for a person.

A specialist replies within 1 business day. If you contact us outside specialist hours, for example on a Saturday, the assistant records the details and the specialist reply arrives on the next business day. You do not need to repeat yourself, because the specialist sees the full conversation.

## What to have ready

- Your order number. It starts with "FH-" and is in your confirmation email.
- The email address on your account.
- Photos, if the request is about damage or a warranty claim.

Never share your password or full card number with support. We will not ask for them.

Specialist hours of 8 am to 6 pm Mountain Time are 7 am to 5 pm Pacific, 9 am to 7 pm Central, and 10 am to 8 pm Eastern.
