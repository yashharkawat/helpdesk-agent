## When you can cancel

You can cancel an order only while its status is "processing". At that stage the order has not left our warehouse, so we can stop it and nothing is charged. Your card is charged when the order ships, which means a cancelled processing order normally results in no charge at all. If you see a pending authorization on your statement, it drops off on its own within a few business days.

Orders move quickly. An order placed before 1 pm Mountain Time ships the same business day, so the cancellation window can be only a few hours. If you need to cancel, contact support as soon as you can. The AI assistant is available 24/7 and can cancel a processing order right away.

## When you cannot cancel

Once the status changes to "shipped", the order cannot be cancelled. We have no way to recall a package from the carrier. You need to wait for it to arrive and then return it under the normal returns policy: within 30 days of delivery for Standard members, or 60 days for Plus members, unused and with tags.

Please do not refuse the delivery. A refused package can take weeks to come back to us and delays your refund.

## How to check your status

Sign in to your account and open "Orders". Each order shows one of four statuses:

- Processing: being picked and packed. Can be cancelled.
- Shipped: handed to the carrier, tracking available. Cannot be cancelled.
- Delivered: marked delivered by the carrier.
- Cancelled: stopped before shipping.

## Cancelling part of an order

While an order is processing, we can cancel the whole order but not individual items. If you want to drop one item, cancel the order and place a new one with the items you want. Changing the shipping address follows the same rule and is only possible while the order is processing.
