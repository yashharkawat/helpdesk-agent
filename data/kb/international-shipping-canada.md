## Where we deliver

Fernhill Outfitters ships only within the United States and Canada. We do not ship to any other country, and we do not accept orders addressed to freight forwarders or package-forwarding services. If you enter an address outside these two countries, checkout will not let you complete the order.

## Delivery times to Canada

Orders to Canada use the same two shipping options as domestic orders, with extra transit time for the border crossing. Canada adds 3-5 business days:

- Standard: 4-6 business days plus 3-5 business days, so roughly 7-11 business days in total.
- Express: 2 business days plus 3-5 business days, so roughly 5-7 business days in total.

Shipping prices are the same as for United States orders. Standard is free over USD 75 and USD 6.95 otherwise. Express is USD 14.95. All prices on our site and all charges to your card are in US dollars. Your bank sets the exchange rate and may add a foreign transaction fee.

## Duties and taxes

Duties and import taxes on orders to Canada are paid by the customer. They are not included in the total you see at checkout, and we cannot estimate or prepay them for you. The carrier usually collects them at the door or sends you a payment link before delivery. If you refuse to pay duties and the package is sent back, we refund the items once they reach our warehouse.

## Returns from Canada

The normal returns policy applies: 30 days from delivery for Standard members and 60 days for Plus members, unused and with tags. Return labels are available for Canadian addresses. Duties you paid to the Canadian authorities are not something we can refund. You may be able to claim them back from the customs agency directly.

Tracking for Canadian orders may pause for a day or two while the package clears customs. This is normal and is not a sign of a lost package.
