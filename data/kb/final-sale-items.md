## What "Final sale" means

Some items are sold as final sale. These are typically clearance items priced to sell through the last of a season's stock. A final-sale item cannot be returned or refunded once you have bought it, regardless of your membership tier. The 30-day Standard window and the 60-day Plus window do not apply to it.

## How to spot a final-sale item

The words "Final sale" appear in three places:

- On the product page, next to the price.
- In your cart, beside the item name, before you pay.
- On your order confirmation email, on the line for that item.

If an item does not carry the "Final sale" label in those places, it follows the normal returns policy. An order can contain a mix of both. In that case only the final-sale lines are excluded, and the other items can still be returned.

## The one exception

If a final-sale item arrives damaged or defective, you are covered. Report it within 14 days of delivery with photos of the damage and your order number. You can choose a replacement or a full refund. If stock has run out, which is common with clearance items, we will refund you instead.

Changing your mind, ordering the wrong size, or finding the color different from what you expected are not exceptions.

## Other rules that do not apply

Final-sale and clearance purchases are not eligible for price adjustments, so if the price drops again after you buy, we do not refund the difference. We also do not offer direct exchanges on any item, so a final-sale item cannot be swapped for another size.

Fernhill-brand gear bought on final sale still carries its warranty against manufacturing defects. See the warranty coverage article for the terms.
