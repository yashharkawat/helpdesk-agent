## What it costs

Fernhill Plus costs USD 39 per year. It renews once a year on the date you joined, and we email a reminder before the renewal. Everyone who is not on Plus is a Standard member, which is free.

## What you get

Plus members receive four benefits:

- 60-day returns. You have 60 days from delivery to return unused items with tags, instead of the Standard 30 days.
- Free return labels. Every return ships on a free prepaid label. Standard members pay a flat USD 7.95 deducted from the refund.
- Free Express shipping on orders over USD 150. Express is 2 business days and normally costs USD 14.95.
- Early access to sales. Plus members can shop sale events before they open to everyone.

Plus does not change the final-sale rule. Items marked "Final sale" cannot be returned or refunded by any member unless they arrive damaged or defective. It also does not change refund approval: refunds above USD 100.00 per order still go to a human specialist.

## Joining

Add Plus at checkout or from the "Membership" page in your account. You can pay with a credit or debit card or PayPal. Fernhill gift cards cannot be used to buy Plus membership. Benefits start right away and apply to orders placed from that moment on.

## Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel Plus at any time from your account or by contacting support. What happens next depends on timing:

- Within the first 14 days, and no Plus benefit used: the USD 39 fee is refundable in full.
- Within the first 14 days, but a benefit was used, such as a free return label or free Express shipping: no refund, and your benefits stay active until the end of the paid year.
- After 14 days: no refund, and your benefits stay active until the end of the paid year.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Approved membership refunds go to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear.
