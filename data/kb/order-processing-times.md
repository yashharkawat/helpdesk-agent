## The 1 pm cut-off

Orders placed before 1 pm Mountain Time ship the same business day. Orders placed at or after 1 pm Mountain Time ship the next business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays, so an order placed on Saturday or Sunday ships on Monday.

Some examples:

- Tuesday 11:30 am Mountain Time: ships Tuesday.
- Tuesday 2:15 pm Mountain Time: ships Wednesday.
- Friday 4 pm Mountain Time: ships Monday.

If you are in another time zone, convert first. 1 pm Mountain Time is 12 pm Pacific, 2 pm Central, and 3 pm Eastern.

## What happens while an order is processing

After you check out, your order has the status "processing". During this stage the warehouse picks your items, checks them, and packs them. This is the only stage where you can cancel the order or change the shipping address. Because same-day orders move fast, that window can be only a few hours long.

Your card is not charged while the order is processing. It is charged when the order ships.

## When the order ships

When the carrier collects the package, the status changes to "shipped" and you get an email with the carrier name and tracking number. Delivery estimates count from this ship date:

- Standard: 4-6 business days.
- Express: 2 business days.
- Canada: add 3-5 business days to either option.

## Possible delays

Processing can take an extra business day during major sales, when we may hold an order briefly to verify a payment, or when an item in your order needs a stock check. If your order has been "processing" for more than 2 business days, contact support with your order number and we will find out why.

Processing time is separate from shipping time. Choosing Express speeds up the carrier leg, not the warehouse cut-off.
