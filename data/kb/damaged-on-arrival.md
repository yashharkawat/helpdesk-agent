## Report it within 14 days

If something in your order arrives damaged or defective, report it within 14 days of delivery. Contact support with:

- Your order number.
- Photos of the damaged item.
- A photo of the shipping box, if the box was crushed, torn, or wet.

Photos are required. They let us approve your claim without asking you to ship anything first, and they help us file a claim with the carrier.

## Your options

You choose how we fix it:

- A replacement, sent at no charge with the same shipping speed as the original order.
- A full refund to your original payment method, which takes 5-7 business days to appear after approval.

If the item is out of stock, we refund you instead. The AI assistant can approve refunds up to USD 100.00 per order on its own. Above USD 100.00, the request goes to a human specialist, who replies within 1 business day.

## Sending the item back

If the order value is under USD 50, you do not need to return the damaged item. Keep it, recycle it, or throw it away.

If the order value is USD 50 or more, we email you a free prepaid return label. Damaged and wrong items always get a free label, whatever your membership tier. You do not need to wait for the damaged item to reach us before a replacement is sent.

## Final-sale items

Damage on arrival is the one exception to the final-sale rule. A final-sale item that arrives damaged or defective qualifies for a replacement or a full refund, as long as you report it within the same 14 days.

If you find a fault later, it may be covered by warranty instead. Fernhill-brand gear has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects, and tents and backpacks have 3 years. Shipping damage reported after 14 days cannot be claimed from the carrier, so please open your package soon after it arrives.
