## Tracking has stopped moving

Short pauses in tracking are normal, especially over weekends, in bad weather, or while a Canadian order clears customs. We treat a package as possibly lost when tracking shows no movement for 7 days. If your tracking has been stuck for 7 days, contact support with your order number and we will start a carrier trace.

## Tracking says "delivered" but you do not have it

Carriers sometimes scan a package as delivered up to a day before it actually arrives. Wait 2 business days, and in the meantime:

- Check around your property: side doors, garage, porch, mail room, parcel lockers.
- Ask neighbors and anyone else at your address.
- Look for a delivery notice from the carrier.
- Confirm the shipping address on your order confirmation email.

If the package has not turned up after 2 business days, contact support.

## What we do next

We open a trace with the carrier, which takes up to 5 business days. The carrier checks its scans, delivery location data, and the driver's notes. You do not need to contact the carrier yourself.

When the trace is finished, and the package has not been found, you choose one of two outcomes:

- A replacement shipped at no charge.
- A refund to your original payment method, which takes 5-7 business days to appear after approval.

Refunds above USD 100.00 per order are approved by a human specialist, who replies within 1 business day. The AI assistant can approve smaller refunds itself.

## If the package shows up later

If the original package arrives after we have sent a replacement or refund, please let us know. We will email a free prepaid label so you can send it back.

If the address you entered was incorrect, the carrier may return the package to us. An address can only be changed while an order is "processing", so please check it carefully at checkout.
