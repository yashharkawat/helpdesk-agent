## When a change is possible

You can change the shipping address on an order only while its status is "processing". At that stage the package has not been labelled or handed to a carrier, so we can update the address before it leaves.

Be quick. Orders placed before 1 pm Mountain Time ship the same business day, so the processing stage can last only a few hours. Contact support as soon as you notice the mistake. The AI assistant is available 24/7 and can update the address on a processing order immediately. Have your order number and the full new address ready, including the unit or apartment number and the postal code.

## When it is too late

Once the status is "shipped", we cannot change the address. The package is with the carrier and we cannot redirect it from our side. Your options are:

- Use the carrier's own tools. UPS, USPS, and FedEx each offer delivery holds or redirects on their tracking pages, sometimes for a fee. This is between you and the carrier, and we cannot guarantee it works.
- Ask someone at the old address to accept the package for you.
- If the package cannot be delivered, the carrier returns it to us and we refund the items once it arrives at our warehouse.

## Limits on address changes

The new address must be within the United States or Canada, because those are the only places we ship. If a change moves the order from the United States to Canada, delivery takes 3-5 business days longer and duties are paid by the customer.

Changing the address does not change your shipping option or its price.

## Updating your saved addresses

Editing the address book in your account only affects future orders. It does not update orders you have already placed. To fix an existing order, you must contact support while it is still processing.
