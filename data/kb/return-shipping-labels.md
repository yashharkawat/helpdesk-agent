## Who pays for return shipping

What you pay to send an item back depends on your membership tier and the reason for the return.

You get a free prepaid label if:

- You are a Plus member. All your returns ship free.
- The item arrived damaged or defective.
- We sent you the wrong item.
- You are returning footwear to get a different size. This applies to everyone, Standard and Plus.

In every other case, Standard members pay a flat USD 7.95 for the return label. You do not pay this up front. It is deducted from your refund, so a USD 68.00 item returned by a Standard member results in a USD 60.05 refund.

## Getting your label

Contact support with your order number and the item you want to send back. Once the return is approved, we email a label as a PDF to the address on your account. Print it, or show the QR code at the carrier's counter if you do not have a printer.

Each label covers one package. If you are returning items from two different orders, you need a separate label for each order.

## Packing and drop-off

Use the original box if you still have it, or any sturdy box or mailer. Remove or cover old shipping labels. Keep the item's tags attached, because returns must be unused with tags. Drop the package at any location for the carrier named on the label and keep the receipt until your refund arrives.

## Using your own carrier

You may ship a return at your own cost with any carrier, but we cannot reimburse that postage and we cannot trace a package we did not label. We recommend using the label we send.

Return labels are valid for shipments within the United States and Canada, the same regions we ship to.
