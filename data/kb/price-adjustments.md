## How it works

If an item you bought goes on sale within 14 days of purchase, Fernhill refunds the difference between what you paid and the new sale price. The 14 days count from the date you placed the order, not the delivery date.

For example, if you bought a rain shell for USD 99.00 and it drops to USD 79.00 nine days later, you can ask for a USD 20.00 price adjustment.

## The rules

- One adjustment per order. If two items on the same order go on sale, ask for both at the same time, because a second request on that order will be declined.
- The item must be the same product, size, and color you bought, and it must be in stock at the lower price when you ask.
- The lower price must be on our own site. We do not match other retailers.
- Final-sale and clearance purchases are not eligible. If you bought an item at a clearance price and it is marked down further, the difference is not refunded.
- The order must not be cancelled or already returned.

## How to ask

Contact support within the 14 days and give your order number and the name of the item. The AI assistant is available 24/7 and can check the current price on the spot. Price adjustments are refunds, so the usual approval limit applies: the assistant can approve up to USD 100.00 per order, and anything above that goes to a human specialist who replies within 1 business day.

## How you are paid

The difference is refunded to your original payment method and takes 5-7 business days to appear after approval. We do not issue price adjustments as store credit or gift cards.

If you miss the 14-day window, the adjustment is not available. You can still return an unused item with tags within your return window and buy it again at the lower price.
